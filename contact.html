Quick answers

How long does delivery take?

Zarvo delivers free across Pakistan. Orders are usually delivered within 3–7 days after confirmation. See Delivery & COD.

How do I pay?

Every order is Cash on Delivery. You pay the courier when your parcel arrives.

Can I return a product?

Eligible products can be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery. See Returns & Exchanges.

How do I check my order status?

Message us on WhatsApp with your Order ID and our team will check your parcel status for you.

Before you message

To help us answer faster, include your Order ID, the product name and a short description of your question. For damaged items, attach clear photos of the product and packaging.
