Delivery & Cash on Delivery

Free delivery across Pakistan, paid in cash when your parcel arrives.

How delivery works

  • Delivery charges: free for every order, everywhere in Pakistan.
  • Payment: Cash on Delivery (COD). You pay the courier when you receive your parcel.
  • Delivery time: usually 3–7 days after your order is confirmed.
  • Order confirmation: our team confirms every order on WhatsApp or by phone before dispatch.

We deliver to homes, shops and offices across Pakistan, including smaller cities and rural areas. If your area is served by a limited courier route, delivery can take a little longer — but our target is a maximum of 7 days.

What happens after you order

  1. You place your order on the website and receive an Order ID on screen.
  2. Our team confirms your order, address and mobile number on WhatsApp or by phone.
  3. Your parcel is dispatched with a courier partner.
  4. The courier delivers to your address and you pay cash on delivery.
Please keep your phone available after ordering. If we cannot reach you to confirm the order, dispatch may be delayed.

Delivery address tips

  • Write your complete address: house/flat number, street, area and city.
  • Add a nearby landmark (mosque, school, shop) to help the courier find you.
  • Double-check your mobile number — it is used for confirmation and delivery calls.

Delayed or missing parcel

If your parcel has not arrived within 7 days of confirmation, message our WhatsApp support with your Order ID. We will check with the courier and update you the same day where possible.
